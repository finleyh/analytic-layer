Data notes
①Two "overseas" tactics, not one. Foreign carriers as upstreams (CO satellite AS265861, DO Servimast / Altice, MX) vs. the operators' own switches hosted abroad (Vultr / Hetzner DE, DigitalOcean GB). The provider flags separate them; a country-only view conflates them and would overstate "foreign carrier" by about half.
②Pattern, not shift — on this data. Every monitored IP's non-US share is flat week over week since Aug 10. The chart that would prove a change needs a longer baseline than the 60-day backfill, or a comparison set of operators who don't do this.
③Upstream ask: peer_country and peer_asn on flow_rollup_hourly. Both are on every raw flow row and dropped by the GROUP BY. Adding them turns this page's trend into a live continuous aggregate and unblocks the By-ASN tab, the graph cluster, and candidate context in one change.
④Geo coverage is cloud-heavy. Lat/lon lives on the provider tables (migration 0069) and the nightly geocode-backfill fills it, but carriers — the interesting ones here — are mostly country-only so far. Country-level views are honest today; dots are not.
⑤What this feeds the queue. A new trunk-role pair in a country the monitored IP has never used before is a one-shot rule over sip_peer_candidates (first_day, peer_country) — the "tactics shift" detector the trend chart can't be yet. And the carrier-vs-node judgment on a confirmed trunk is the analysis-pillar call that stays human (or agent) — the heuristic deliberately stops short of it.
